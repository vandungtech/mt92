## Training and quality

Stage one trained a rank-32 LoRA for three epochs on 4,430 public examples,
holding out 384 deterministic examples. It scored `0.9304029304` entity
micro-F1 over all 384, with zero malformed outputs. Stage two performed one
low-rate epoch on all 4,814 usable public examples to strengthen quantization
margins.

On the same 64-example comparison slice, stage two scored:

| Runtime | Entity micro-F1 | Malformed | Mean latency |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| BF16 Transformers | 0.980695 | 0 | GPU benchmark |
| Q4_K_M validator engine | 0.961240 | 0 | 3591 ms |
| Q3_K_M validator engine | 0.921875 | 0 | 4484 ms |

The 64 examples were included in stage two, so those numbers compare
quantizations but are not an unbiased generalization estimate. The earlier
384-example score is the honest held-out result.
